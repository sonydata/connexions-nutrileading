## Project rules
- Session content comes from the local bank in `src/lib/content.ts`, assembled by rules in `src/lib/builder.ts` — no AI per session, to keep costs low and facts controlled.
- Exercise photos come only from the curated library in `src/lib/library.ts`.
- Visual hints pair an explicit semantic cue with a precise curated photo when available, shared by all response modes and revealed only on request; never substitute a generic image for a missing subject or change scoring.
- Spoken audio is synthesised once per sentence and cached in the private `voice` storage bucket — repeat plays cost nothing.
- Adaptation is computed per skill from recent evaluated `attempts` at session start. Knowledge turns (a choice question or a word to find) are saved with their exercise kind and a silent outcome: spontaneous, after_cue (with help or via choices), after_repeat (spoken but not recognised), revealed — never shown to the person. Opinion/repeat turns stay neutral `discussion:` rows, excluded from level and affinity inference.
- Every knowledge turn ends with the answer said aloud (confirmText / revealText in discussion.ts), never "wrong".
- "Accompagnement renforcé" (`params.reinforced`, from the caregiver profile, automatic when comprehension ≥ 4) shows the simple screen: big start button, picture + question, choices or hands-free listening that ends after a pause, answer said, automatic next turn. Caregiver controls (Réécouter, J'ai fini, Passer) stay small at the bottom.
- Date-based items (`datedReperes`) are built at session time, never at module load (the server clock reads 1970 at load). When Actualité is chosen, one dated Repère question opens every session (least recently seen first); the chat about today's date appears at most every 3 days. Never ask orientation questions (year, month, season, today's president) — Repères are always dated facts ("élu en 2024").
- Personal targets (spaced retrieval + errorless learning, `src/lib/spaced-retrieval.ts`): the caregiver enters text-only facts (sentence / question / answer — never photos) stored in `adaptive_profiles.answers.personal`. One target per session: stated on the first ask, then asked again after GAPS activities (longer after success, back after a miss, stop after two first misses); saved as `attempts` kind `srt`, `option_count` = step, excluded from bank levels and dashboard percentages. Their sentences are voiced only for that account. The helper's "C'était juste" overrides a missed recognition.
- Spoken answers use the free browser SpeechRecognition (fr-FR) as an indicative signal only, never a score; recordings stay in browser memory and are discarded after each exercise — no paid transcription, no upload.
- Oral rows in `attempts` reuse existing columns: `kind` = oral mode, `option_count` = approximate spoken word count, `concept` suffixed `#rep` when the model sentence was repeated — avoids a schema change.
- Choice photos are shown only when every option has one (the file must exist, not just the key), and never for advice/action answers, so an image never contradicts the answer; otherwise a context photo from `SCENE` illustrates the situation, never the answer. `SCENE[id] = ""` means deliberately no photo. Hint photos (`hint-*`) and portraits (`p-*`) are never matched by keyword.
- Personalities (Actualité) are offered as photo + name once a freely licensed portrait is added as `src/assets/library/p-<name>.jpg`; until then names only.
- Guided sessions start directly in one blended flow; no mode selection is shown because it was confusing. Continuing never requires validation and speech is never evaluated.
- Every knowledge turn (choice, Vrai/Faux, word to find, missing word, picture to name) is answered by touching a choice shown from the start (feedback Sonia 15 Oct: not always aloud). Speaking stays possible but optional. Word choices come from explicit `distractors`/`wd`, else words of the same kind and subject. A right tap without help is "spontaneous"; after a hint, "after_cue". Only the right answer gets a tick; another choice is dimmed, never marked wrong.
- No open follow-up question after each answer: the answer is said, then Continuer. Opinion turns are the first activities dropped when a session is shortened.
- Every session contains at least one "Écoutez, puis répétez la phrase" turn; the sentence shown, heard and repeated is the same one (never a paraphrase).
- Instructions are one short action: "Cliquez sur la bonne réponse." for every question with choices / "Écoutez, puis répétez la phrase." / "Donnez votre avis à voix haute.".
- Strict interest matching: every activity of a session belongs to a chosen interest, or is transversal (`general`: time, proverbs). The new path, reactivated words and quick bank questions all stay within the chosen interests (tested).
- Response-mode instructions live in a shared browser-safe module used by the session and voice inventory; each prompt names one concrete action without numbered answer announcements.
- Discovery mode: /seance runs without an account — built locally, nothing saved.
- Voice: one natural voice, whole sentences only (never split into fragments). A missing clip is synthesised once and cached — only for sentences of the bank (`isVoiceText` in voice-texts.ts, plus "A, B ou C ?" sentences made only of known answer choices), for guests and accounts alike, so cost stays bounded. No money for credits (Sonia, 15 Oct): the free device voice (`device-voice.ts`, most natural French voice first, choosable in Espace aidant › VoicePicker, saved per device) is used for the WHOLE session either when chosen ("Toujours la voix de l'appareil") or as soon as a recorded sentence is unavailable — never a mix within a session. All sentences of a turn are requested together (max 2 voice requests at once). Choices are said as one sentence in a stable order (`seededShuffle`, `trimOptions`). The caregiver's "Préparer la voix" (`prepareVoice`) records every sentence in advance and shows why it stops (e.g. Lovable AI credits).
- Numbers are written as digits everywhere ("42 kilomètres", "19e siècle"); number answers are shown big.
- Every bank item has a subject (`topic`, explicit or derived by topicOf) separate from its skill; buildSession fills ~80 % of slots from the chosen interests (rotating subjects) and ~20 % from transversal "general" items — personalisation stays rule-based.
- First launch goes through /interets; signed-in choices are saved in caregiver_settings.topics ("i:" prefix), guest choices in localStorage.
- Sessions are built from thematic mini-sequences in `src/lib/sequences.ts` (Comprendre → Retrouver → S'exprimer → Reformuler on one subject), 3 per session + 1 time item — keeps choice questions ≈ a quarter and gives continuity; the old slot plan is only a fallback.
- Sessions are built by buildPlan in builder.ts: one familiar path (a series already begun, or a liked subject), one new path, quick questions drawn from the whole bank on the chosen subjects (least recently seen first), one sentence to repeat, one proverb, a time item and one earlier word brought back with spacing (2 days if it gave trouble, else 7). Each item has a `drop` priority used by `discussionTurns(items, mode, limit)` to shorten to `maxTurns`. Rules only, so retention costs nothing and stays predictable.
- Memory games live at /memoire (pairs, missing object, shopping list): no timer, no score, nothing saved, library photos with their names.
- Collections in sequences.ts are ordered and double as multi-day series. "Explored" means the path's "-c" step was answered, so no schema change is needed.
- Adaptation goes through `src/lib/adaptive-profile.ts` (`deriveParams`): caregiver functional profile (`adaptive_profiles.answers`) + recent `signal:*`/`discussion:*` rows → session params; language support and intellectual complexity stay separate — single, pure place to evolve the algorithm.
- Interaction signals (repeat, not understood, abandon) are `attempts` rows with `kind` = `signal:<name>`, excluded from levels/affinity and scores — no schema change, no audio stored.
- Home page "Cette semaine" shows positive counts only (sessions, minutes, answers found, sentences repeated, opinions shared, subjects explored), never a percentage. No "Vos collections" block on the home page (removed 15 Oct).
- Espace aidant stays short: En bref (3 figures + 14-day strip, detail folded), Cette semaine observations, personal landmarks, profile (with voice test), one Réglages card (name, interests, starting level).
- Photos: a file named after a question (`<item id>.jpg`) or a path (`sq-….jpg`) in src/assets/library is used automatically and wins over SCENE. A main photo never shows the answer (`notTheAnswer`); no photo rather than a loose one.
- Hints (`hintFor`) never contain the answer (tested): meaning cue, first sound of the word, or one wrong choice removed (only with 3+ choices). Hint pictures only for word items, never the answer.
- Voice order: knowledge turn = context, question, the choices read aloud as one sentence with pauses ("La banane… ou… la pomme ?", Vrai/Faux too), THEN "Cliquez sur la bonne réponse.". Repeat turn = "Écoutez, puis répétez la phrase." → sentence → "Répétez la phrase à voix haute.". A right answer is praised aloud (PRAISE, pre-recorded) then confirmed. Prefer already-recorded sentences for instructions: each new sentence costs one synthesis (Lovable AI credits) and stays silent until made.
- "Jeux de mémoire" is also an interest (`memoire`, stored like the others, never a subject for questions): when ticked, `buildPlan` returns `game` (rotates daily) and the session shows that memory game in the middle as its own step (`gameTurn`), never recorded. Games live in components/memory-games.tsx, shared with /memoire.
- Personalised pictures (feedback 15 Oct): each question (bank item) and each path (`sq-…`, plus its "-r" step) can have its own picture generated once with Lovable AI from its own text (`pictures.ts`: `pictureJobs`, `picturePrompt` — the subject, never the answer, no text, no recognisable real person), stored in the public `pictures` bucket by key. The session shows it first (`QuestionPicture`), else the library photo. The caregiver's "Préparer les images" (`preparePictures`) makes the missing ones, two at a time, and says why it stops.
- Voice requests: the current turn is requested first; the next turn is prepared only after it. When the natural voice cannot be made, the screen says why (`voiceReason`), and "Tester la voix" also tries an unrecorded sentence.
